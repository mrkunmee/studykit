Disclaimer

StudentKit Nigeria is here to help you study. To use it well, it helps to know exactly what it can and cannot do.

Educational purpose

Everything on StudentKit — the tools and the guides — is provided for general educational help. It is not professional, academic, or legal advice.

Calculator assumptions

Each tool works out a result from the numbers you enter and the options you choose (for example, a grading scale or assessment weights). It does not know your institution's exact rules, so it cannot account for policies it has not been told about.

Grading systems differ

Nigerian universities and polytechnics use different grade points and class boundaries, and these can change over time. Any grading example shown on the site is an illustration on a stated scale, not a universal standard.

Limits of planning and estimates

Study timetables and exam-score figures are planning aids. They help you organise and set targets, but they are not promises about what will happen.

Accuracy and verification

We aim for correct calculations and accurate content, and the worked examples are checked. Even so, always verify anything that affects an important decision — such as your official results or graduation status — with your institution or the relevant official source.

No guarantee of results

Nothing on StudentKit guarantees academic performance or examination outcomes. A practice quiz score, in particular, does not predict your result in any official examination.

No affiliation

StudentKit Nigeria is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to JAMB, WAEC, NECO, any university or polytechnic, or any other examination body. Any such relationship would only be stated here if it were independently established.
