Study Planner

A study timetable turns "I'll study later" into specific sessions. Enter your subjects and the time you actually have; the planner spreads your subjects across your free time. It runs on your device and nothing is saved to a server.

    How sessions are allocated

    Each subject is given a weight from its priority (High = 3, Medium = 2, Low = 1). A subject with an exam date in the next 14 days gets +1. Sessions are then spread smoothly across your available slots in proportion to those weights, so higher-priority and sooner subjects appear more often — without clustering. "Generate another arrangement" rotates the order to give a different valid plan from the same inputs.

    Saved on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    Building a balanced timetable

    Give more sessions to harder, heavier, or sooner subjects, but keep every subject in the plan so nothing is neglected. Short breaks between sessions help you keep focus, and reviewing your plan each week keeps it realistic. For the full method, read the guide.
