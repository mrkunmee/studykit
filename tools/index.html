All tools

Five free tools for everyday academic tasks. Each one runs in your browser and gives an estimate you can read, print and verify.

CGPA Calculator

Enter your courses, credit units and grades on a scale you choose. Get your semester GPA and, if you add previous totals, your cumulative CGPA.

Study Planner

Tell it your subjects and free time; it lays out a day-by-day study timetable you can print.

Practice Quiz

Pick a subject and length, answer original questions, then review which ones you missed and why.

Exam Score Planner

Percentage Calculator

Three clearly-labelled modes for the percentage maths students need most.

Open Percentage Calculator →

How to choose

  • Checking your grades? → CGPA Calculator
  • Organising your study time? → Study Planner
  • Testing what you know? → Practice Quiz
  • Working towards a target mark? → Exam Score Planner
  • Quick percentage maths? → Percentage Calculator
