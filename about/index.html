About StudentKit Nigeria

Our purpose

StudentKit Nigeria is a free collection of practical study tools and guides for Nigerian students. The goal is simple: useful tools that work well on your phone, with clear results and honest limitations. It is not a blog farm, a school portal, or an advertising-first website — it is a small toolkit that does a few things well.

Who it's for

It is built for Nigerian university and polytechnic students: anyone preparing for examinations, organising study time, checking their CGPA, or needing quick, reliable academic tools on a phone or computer.

What we offer

Five tools and three guides, each focused on one job:

  • CGPA Calculator — semester GPA and cumulative CGPA on a scale you choose.
  • Study Planner — a day-by-day study timetable from your free time.
  • Practice Quiz — original practice questions with answer review.
  • Exam Score Planner — the mark you need to reach a target.
  • Percentage Calculator — three clear percentage modes.

The guides explain the thinking behind the tools so the numbers make sense.

Our commitments

  • Clarity. Plain language and results you can actually read.
  • Usefulness. Tools that do one job well and run entirely in your browser.
  • Transparency. Results are estimates based on your inputs — always confirm official rules with your institution. We are not affiliated with JAMB, WAEC, NECO, or any university.

Who runs StudentKit

StudentKit Nigeria is operated by Study Kit ng, an independent project. We do not invent credentials, partnerships, or endorsements, and we are not affiliated with any examination body or institution.
