Overview

StudentKit Nigeria is a website of study tools and guides. The tools run entirely in your browser. This policy explains what information is and is not handled when you use the site.

Information we collect

StudentKit does not ask you to create an account and has no database of its own. The tools (CGPA calculator, study planner, quiz, exam planner and percentage calculator) process the values you type on your device; those values are not transmitted to us.

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like most hosting providers, Vercel may automatically process standard technical information (such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested) in its server logs to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the site. See Vercel's own privacy documentation for details of its processing.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address you send from and whatever you choose to include in your message, so we can reply. The site itself has no contact form that stores or transmits messages.

Local storage on your device

Some tools can save your entries on your own device using your browser's local storage, so your work is still there when you return — for example your CGPA course list or a generated study timetable. This data:

  • stays in your browser on your device;
  • is not sent to us or to anyone else;
  • can be removed at any time using the "Clear saved data" / "Clear saved plan" button in the tool, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Cookies and analytics

As built, StudentKit sets no cookies of its own and includes no third-party analytics. If analytics or any cookie-setting service is added later, this section will be updated to name it and describe the data involved before that service goes live.

Advertising

Advertising is not active on StudentKit at this time. We intend to apply to a third-party advertising programme (such as Google AdSense) in the future. If and when advertising is enabled, this policy will be updated first to disclose the advertising provider, the use of advertising cookies or identifiers, and links to the provider's policies and your choices for ad personalisation. No advertising code is present until that update is made.

Data retention and security

Because the tools do not send your inputs to us, we do not retain them. Anything saved locally remains on your device until you clear it. Email you send us is kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry. Server logs are handled by our hosting provider, Vercel, under its own retention practices. We take reasonable care, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

  • Clear your locally saved tool data at any time (see "Local storage" above).
  • Use the tools without providing any personal details — no account is required.
  • Contact us about any information you have sent us by email.
  • If advertising or analytics are added later, you will be able to use the relevant provider's controls to limit personalisation; details will be added here at that time.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, use the Contact page or email studykitng@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The "last updated" date above will reflect the latest version.
